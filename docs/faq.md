# FAQ

## How do I finish the game?
Reach 100% productivity on the Productivity Machine. A victory overlay appears at the top of the screen; you can then restart from the pause menu.

## What triggers burnout?
When player health reaches 0, the game auto-pauses and shows the burnout screen (Skyline Watch). Use Continue to recover and return to the corp office.

## How do puzzles work?
The toggle room uses a progressive toggle puzzle. Interact near toggles with `[E]`. After solving, inputs are disabled to prevent accidental presses.

## Where is volume saved?
Volume changes persist to `user://settings.cfg` via `GlobalAudio`. They load on startup and apply to the Master bus.

## Is there a tutorial?
There is no formal tutorial. Explore the corp office and the toggle room to learn mechanics quickly.
