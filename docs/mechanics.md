# Game Mechanics

## Leadership Points
Leadership points are your primary resource. Use them to:
- Hire teammates.
- Build defenses.

## Defense Types
- **Coffee Machine**: Heals stress by 20 points. Costs 1 leadership point.
- **Standing Desk**: Reduces stress by 14 points. Costs 5 leadership points.

## Waves
Each wave progresses your project. Complete all waves to finish a project.
